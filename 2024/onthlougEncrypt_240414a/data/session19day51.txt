ONLY FOR THE TONGUES OF THE FAITHFUL OF ONTHLOUG

those who would pry into the secrets of our order
	you do so at peril
		to your life
		to your power
those who have been ascended
	we welcome you freely
	and share openly the secrets of knowledge we have found
those who follow our path in our demise
	may you seek the blessings of our lord onthloug
	whose care and ownership we are all entrusted to

MAY HIS TENDER EMBRACE PROTECT US ALL

THE NINETEENTH EXCURSION
on the fifty first day of our campaign
	we followed our lord's demands
a dream led us to aid our brethren
	deep in the thick woods of moerheb weald
much of this is detailed elsewhere
	but it is not the full story

DISERTH
in the lawless border town of diserth we found a set of old stones
	arrayed in a circle
they tasted of respect
	and fear
	and mystery
those stones were a meeting place for the faithful
	perhaps they can be again

THE VILLAGE OF COWARDS
we came to a village
	protected by tongues on the trees surrounding it
they expected the procession of the wise
	and were fearful of us
they are wrong-hearted to be fearful
the children
	perhaps knowing who we all ultimately serve
		directed us to our allies
the adults sent us away
	a horrible look in their eyes
twice-born scried and saw
	children partaking of a ritual
	water burst out of them
		they were robed and taken away

THE CIRCLE OF THE WEIGHTED TONGUES
our beloved
	who are also kept by his embrace
greeted us first with suspicion
	but then glad tidings
		knowing we are sent by HIS WILL
they have a curious tradition
	they pierce their tongues
	with weights
		letting them hang down
		they flaunt openly the beauty of our gift
		they show openly his power
			there is beauty in this
they find themselves under threat
	from the christians at the monastery
	of the so-called saint glanyrafor
our brother who was then only twice-born
	looked from a distance at the monastery
		and saw weakness
we devised a plan
	the dragon intervened
